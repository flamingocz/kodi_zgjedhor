WHAT THE INITIATIVE AIMS TO CHANGE

More participation.
Greater accountability.

Fewer electoral districts and fully open lists

Reducing the number of electoral districts and using 100% open lists for members of Parliament and municipal councils.

Citizen lists with independent candidates

Giving citizens a way to enter the electoral contest through citizen lists with independent candidates.

Depoliticised election commissions and counting groups

Depoliticising and removing party control from election commissions and vote-counting groups.

Home voting for voters with disabilities

Creating the possibility of voting at home for voters with disabilities, under the proposed procedures.

Fewer opportunities for abuse and stronger penalties

Reducing the use of public assets, pressure on voters and illicit money, while strengthening penalties for violations.

Overview based on the supplied English leaflet. Proposal details are checked against the Albanian draft. Participation instructions below reflect the campaign’s confirmed guidance.

The initiative needs at least 20,000 supporting signatures to be submitted to Parliament. This is the required number, not a count of signatures collected.

How to complete the form

If you are unsure: stop and contact the WhatsApp group — do not improvise.

  1. 1
    Complete only the designated columns.

    Do not write in other parts of the form.

  2. 2
    Check the eligibility requirements.

    The signer must be an Albanian citizen, aged 18 or over, eligible to vote and listed in Albania’s voter register. They must show a valid Albanian identity card or passport. Check the expiry date and make sure the document belongs to the person in front of you.

  3. 3
    Complete all information in capital letters.

    Write clearly and complete every field, using the Albanian identity document. If you make a mistake, use another row; if a new official sheet cannot be used, draw a line through the incorrect row.

  4. 4
    Use a pen and check the handwriting.

    The authorised collector copies the signer’s details carefully from the Albanian identity document. The signer checks the written details and signs personally. Use capital letters, clear handwriting and complete every field.

  5. 5
    Check the ID without photographing or photocopying it.

    UNDER “ID NUMBER,” WRITE THE NUMBER FROM THE ALBANIAN IDENTITY CARD OR PASSPORT.

    DO NOT ENTER THE PERSONAL NUMBER.

  6. 6
    Add the country code to contact numbers outside Albania.

    For example: +39, +44 or +1.

  7. 7
    The signature is personal.

    The signer signs only once. Nobody may sign on behalf of another person, including a family member.

  8. 8
    Make a final check.

    Before leaving, verify that every field is complete, accurate, legible and free of corrections.

* The contact number allows the CEC, when necessary, to contact the signer to confirm the expressed intention. A phone photo, scan or photocopy of the ID does not replace checking the document.

ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Veprimi Qytetar

“Veprimi Qytetar për Demokraci Funksionale” (Citizen Action for Functional Democracy) brings together citizens and civil-society representatives around the initiative to amend Albania’s Electoral Code. The proposal seeks reform shaped through direct citizen participation.

The draft and information materials on this website are part of that initiative. Citizens can contribute by learning about the proposal, signing the paper form or helping collect signatures.

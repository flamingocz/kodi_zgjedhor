WHAT THE INITIATIVE AIMS TO CHANGE

More participation.
Greater accountability.

Open lists and citizen candidates

Greater weight for preferential votes and citizen lists of independent candidates, creating opportunities to participate outside party structures.

Independent election administration

More professional election management, with training, certification and selection by lot.

Political finance and public resources

Stronger oversight of campaign finance, the use of public resources and electoral violations.

Voter registers and overseas voting

Improvements to voter registers, postal voting arrangements and digital electoral processes.

Accessible voting

Better access for voters with disabilities, including mobile ballot boxes and support services.

Citizen involvement

The initiative seeks reform shaped through participation by citizens and civil society. The full provisions are available in the Albanian draft.

SIGNATURES REQUIRED

20,000

The initiative needs at least 20,000 supporting signatures to be submitted to Parliament. This is the required number, not a count of signatures collected.

How to complete the form

  • Fill in only the designated fields; do not write in other parts of the sheet.
  • Look at the identity document without photographing or photocopying it. Under “ID number,” copy the number from the Albanian identity card or passport.
  • The contact number allows the CEC to confirm the signer’s expressed intention if it considers this necessary. Add the country code to numbers outside Albania.
  • If you make a mistake, do not erase or overwrite it. Use another row or sheet and ask the team if you are unsure.
  • Do not leave the form unattended or pressure anyone; the signature must be freely given, without money, gifts or benefits.
  • Keep the completed form secure and send the signed material only through the initiative’s delivery channels.

Three ways to deliver your signed form

  1. Through a local collector. Give the printed, signed form to a contact person where you live. After collecting the forms, they post them to Tirana at the address provided by the team.
  2. Directly by post. Post the signed form to Albania yourself. Contact the team for the postal address and delivery arrangements.
  3. By email, after signing on paper. Download and print the form, complete the required fields and sign it by hand. Then scan the signed form and attach it to an email to veprimi.qytetar@gmail.com.

ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Veprimi Qytetar

“Veprimi Qytetar për Demokraci Funksionale” (Citizen Action for Functional Democracy) brings together citizens and civil-society representatives around the initiative to amend Albania’s Electoral Code. The proposal seeks reform shaped through direct citizen participation.

The draft and information materials on this website are part of that initiative. Citizens can contribute by learning about the proposal, signing the paper form or helping collect signatures.
